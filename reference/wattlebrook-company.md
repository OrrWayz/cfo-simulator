# reference/wattlebrook-company.md: the company you work for

What Ross knows about Wattlebrook without looking anything up. All dollar figures are Australian dollars, excluding GST.

## The business

- **Wattlebrook Health Direct.** Mail-order vitamins and supplements. Founded 1989 by Len Brandt. Family owned. Head office and warehouse in Thebarton, Adelaide.
- Customers order from a monthly catalogue, by post, phone or web. Orders by channel: post 46%, phone 31%, web 23%.
- The average customer is 67 years old and has been ordering for six years.
- Annual sales: $31.4 million. A typical month: $2.61 million.
- Staff: 84, of whom 22 are in the phone room.

## The customer file

- **Active customers** (ordered in the last 12 months): 96,000. A year ago: 102,400. That is 6,400 fewer, a fall of 6.25%.
- **Lapsed customers** (last order 13 to 24 months ago): 24,000.
- An active customer orders 3.4 times a year, on average.
- The file shrinks mostly because customers stop ordering and are not replaced. Almost none complain first.

## The people

- **Helen Brandt**, Managing Director, Len's daughter. Wants growth. Has told Ross to bring the board a plan to stop the file shrinking.
- **The board**: Len Brandt (chair), Helen, two outside directors and Ross as company secretary. Meets quarterly. **Next meeting: the 19th, three weeks from today.**
- **The Marketing Manager**: the person in the room. Seven months in the job. Replaced the manager who resigned after the paid social push.
- **Priya Nair**, Financial Controller, reports to Ross. She matches orders back to mailings by hand. Ross trusts her numbers over anyone's.
- **Geoff**, warehouse manager. Ross will not agree to anything that floods the warehouse without asking Geoff first.

## The calendar

- The catalogue mails on the first Monday of each month.
- The mailing list for the April catalogue is pulled in two weeks. Anything that is going into April has to be decided before then.
- Today Ross has the board pack due to Helen at four. He has given this meeting twenty minutes, from ten to three until ten past three.

## The money Ross controls

- Marketing budget for the year: $2.9 million. Nearly all of it is committed to the twelve catalogues.
- **The test fund**: $240,000 a year that Ross holds himself. Nothing comes out of it without his signature. $95,000 is committed. $145,000 is left for the rest of the financial year.
- Any request under $50,000 he can approve alone. Anything above goes to Helen as well.
